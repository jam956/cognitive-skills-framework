# Dynamical Prism: separate what happens from what we call it

Textbook chapter: [The Dynamical Prism](../chapters/05_DYNAMICAL_PRISM.md).

**Question:** What process is actually operating?

## The problem and the method

Broad labels such as control, growth or cooperation can make an explanation sound complete before its mechanism has been described. The Dynamical Prism asks which actions or processes produce that behavior and which participants carry them.

This also supports inquiry from the other direction: start with a process and ask what kinds of systems could realize it. The skill makes this choice explicit instead of depending on the model to discover a useful decomposition unaided.

## Historical development

Recovered April 20, 2026 discussions describe a move toward constructing candidate systems from ontology, dynamics or both. The Coupler connected those descriptions to actors and relationships.

The May 6 refinement separated a reduced Dynamical Core from the Regime Field. Processes such as signaling, gating or coordinating belong to the core; scarcity, legitimacy or emergency qualify their operation. Composite behavior labels arise from their combination.

The preserved filename includes v1.2, while its declared reduced-core version is v0.2.0. [Version notes](../PROVENANCE.md) explain that naming history.

## The earlier dialectical movements

Earlier discussions also used thesis, antithesis, paradox, escape and synthesis to describe how an inquiry could move. Those are transformations of the analysis, rather than additional primitive actions in the reduced core. In the current package, related moves are named by CTR and explored by ETE.

The [circle-and-tilt example](../chapters/09_THE_CIRCLE_AND_THE_TILT.md) follows one such movement while keeping the original subject and constraints visible.

## Example and boundary

“Centralized control” might involve receiving information, granting permission and gating execution. The same actions can serve different purposes under emergency authorization or routine administration. A label alone does not establish either the process or its legitimacy.

The primitive vocabulary is a proposed modeling choice. It should not erase source meaning merely to fit a predefined operator.
