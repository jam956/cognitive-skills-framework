# PSPP: decide what the exploration has earned

The archive now has several proposals. One protects the originals by severely restricting handling. Another serves some readers with reproductions. A third combines reproductions with supervised access for questions about the physical object.

The combined proposal sounds promising. It may also require staff the archive does not have. PSPP keeps that practical difficulty from disappearing beneath the appeal of a neat synthesis.

## Comparing what survives

Each candidate returns to the same opening concern: meaningful study that does not exhaust the collection. A comparison asks what each arrangement actually supports, which constraints it violates and what evidence could change the choice.

A reproduction-only arrangement may serve textual questions while failing material ones. Unrestricted original handling may satisfy present access at an unacceptable preservation cost. A supervised arrangement may remain conditional on capacity. These are different judgments, not three scores for the elegance of the prose.

A result can therefore be a selection, a conditional combination or a decision to defer. The initial direct approach remains in consideration unless it fails materially.

## Synthesis is an outcome to earn

The earlier circle placed thesis and antithesis across from one another, retained their tension as a paradox and used a YZ tilt to seek another representation. In the [archive example](../DIALECTICAL_SEARCH.md), that representation distinguishes kinds of access.

PSPP's job is not to reward the branch labeled synthesis. It compares the resulting arrangements. If the new distinction does not support a viable choice, the conflict remains. A clearer unresolved tension can be more useful than an unsupported resolution.

## How comparison became its own job

Parallel problem-solving preceded the current comparator. April 2026 descriptions shifted from alternative answer framings toward candidate constructed systems, with ordered path checks where needed.

The May 2 refactor made PSPP v5.0-lite primarily a comparator: [ETE](ETE.md) explores, PSPP compares, [CREM](CREM.md) handles relationship rebase and [CEC](CEC.md) checks consequential dependencies. This limits duplicated work and makes selection grounds easier to inspect.

Numerical fields in the specification are proposed controls. A model assigning a number has not established calibrated quality or feasibility. Evidence and task-specific checks remain necessary.
