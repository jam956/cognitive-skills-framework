# Regime Field: keep the conditions that change meaning or fit

Textbook chapter: [The Regime Field](../../docs/chapters/06_REGIME_FIELD.md).

**Question:** Under what conditions does this process produce the behavior we observe?

## The problem and the method

A model may transfer a relationship while silently carrying over assumptions about trust, authority, resources or timing. The Regime Field gives those conditions an explicit home, so they can be checked separately from the action.

The compact Regime Profile passes selected constraints between stages. The fuller field defines axes and candidate bundles when more detailed analysis is useful.

## Historical development

Regime was already part of the sentence-thought representation. The May 2026 refinement made its separation from the Dynamical Core more explicit: an action and the conditions under which it operates should not be treated as the same kind of thing. The May 6 source update records this reorganization.

That changed the interpretation of apparent conflict. Two parties may use similar operations under different permissions or constraints; different behavior labels need not imply different primitive processes.

## Example and boundary

Sharing a record can mean the same action under public-release authorization or under a confidentiality restriction. A transferable method must retain the condition that determines whether sharing is permissible.

In a sentence-thought encoding, logical qualifications can also be represented as regime constraints, provided their scope remains explicit. “Not permitted to express a preference” must not become “does not have that preference.” Categorization alone does not enforce that distinction.
